## Context

Wexford Bus runs coach services between Wexford, Dublin City and Dublin Airport. It wanted to refresh its online presence and bring in a new app and online booking system.

The aim was to overhaul the experience and the visuals together, with a distinctive visual language that would stand out from competitors.

## The problem

Wexford Bus wanted more passengers booking online, and fewer calling with questions. The project goals were to:

- **Deliver a fully responsive booking system**
- **Give a seamless experience across mobile, tablet and desktop**
- **Increase web traffic and online bookings**
- **Reduce phone calls and queries,** by making the process intuitive

## What I did

### Research first

We began with foundational research, to immerse the whole team in the world of travel and online booking. We studied best-in-class booking systems and their journeys; Work&Co's redesign for Virgin America was a particular inspiration, technically, functionally and in the experience it created.

We interviewed customers and reviewed the strengths and weaknesses of direct and international competitors. From that we built a set of personas, so the needs of every kind of passenger were accounted for, then mapped the customer journey and planned the user flow.

### Three steps, one timeline

On desktop, the core booking process came down to three key steps, to reduce cognitive load: choose your tickets, add passenger information, pay. A numbered timeline at the top shows passengers where they are and what's left, a tunnelling approach that keeps them focused on finishing. The last step hands over to a secure third-party payment system.

### Booking at the centre of every screen

Booking is the core task, so it sits centre stage on the homepage, on every device. Below it, a feed of three easily updated panels carries offers and news. User interviews identified eight key areas of interest, which became quick links in the footer of every page.

## Outcome

After launch:

- **19% more tickets bought** in the first six months, with the simpler booking process
- **23% more traffic** on average, organic and direct, from better SEO and site structure
- **12% higher conversion rate,** from a more streamlined booking process
- **29% fewer web-related phone calls,** thanks to a more intuitive interface

You can see the live site at [wexfordbus.com](https://www.wexfordbus.com).
