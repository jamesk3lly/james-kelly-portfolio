## Context

eir is one of Ireland's leading providers of broadband, mobile and TV. Its website gets more than 20,000 visitors a week, and the homepage is where most of them start.

The site needed to showcase and communicate the full range of eir's services far better than it did. I led the UX and UI design of the new homepage over eight months in 2020, working with eir's strategy and digital teams and an agile development team.

## The problem

User testing made it clear that visitors to the site weren't aware of the services eir offered. The homepage was a sequence of large promotional banners, each competing for attention, with no clear route to broadband, TV, mobile or support for existing customers.

We needed clearer signposting to communicate what was on offer, and to find and fix the pain points along each of the main customer journeys.

## What I did

I ran the project end to end: discovery workshops and requirement gathering, competitive research, user testing, low and high fidelity wireframes and prototypes, then close work with the development team through build, QA and A/B testing.

### Testing with real customers

Testing identified the existing pain points and kept the design honest as it evolved. The solution came down to four goals:

- **Inform customers:** help people understand the full range of what eir offers
- **Signposts and shortcuts:** help people get to their destination quickly and easily
- **Introduce brand and emotion:** express the eir brand in a more cohesive, consistent way
- **Establish a design system:** bring consistency to every communication

### Mobile first

65% of visitors came to eir.ie on a mobile device, so the homepage was designed for phones first: shortcut tiles near the top, offers in a simple two-column grid, and a dedicated section for mobile phone deals.

### A design system for consistency

To stop each new promotion looking different from the last, I defined a design system for the homepage: a type scale, a colour palette, card components with set character limits, and button styles.

## Outcome

We validated the new homepage with A/B testing against the old one. Over the course of the test:

- **14% increase in transactions**
- **11% more time spent on the site**
- **6% lower bounce rate**

The homepage now serves more than 20,000 visitors a week. You can see the live site at [eir.ie](https://www.eir.ie).
